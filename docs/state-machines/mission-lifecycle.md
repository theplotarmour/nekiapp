# Mission lifecycle transitions

**Status:** Review draft. [Shared command rules](README.md) apply to every row. Sources: TDD §6.1; data model §4.4; PRD FR-06/15/17; flows F7/F8/F10; plan G14/G15/G17/G29.

Preserve all 17 existing mission enum values during review. Proposed semantics: `FUNDING` for money-only needs, `RECRUITING` for time-only needs, `ACTIVE` for item-only or mixed needs (PD-07). These are collection phases, not execution or proof verification. `PUBLISHED` is a committed moderation result with a recoverable classification step. Never determine all CTA availability from the mission state alone.

Every row requires expected version and shared atomic history/audit/outbox. “Eligible org” means current VERIFIED and valid under the reviewed organization policy. Default additional state failure is `409 MISSION_STATE_CONFLICT`. Row-specific errors supplement shared errors. All operational overrides require a scoped actor and reason.

“Collection state” in M23 means exactly FUNDING, RECRUITING or ACTIVE. Changes during other states are denied until a reviewed contract defines them. M23 changes version even when status is unchanged. Ordinary draft edits use the same version/audit rules and may not bypass moderation by altering an approved submission.

## Invariants and terminal behavior

- Mission completion does not create impact at capture time. M12 persists an issuance intent; idempotent impact issuance and uniqueness belong to G17/NK-DOC-3.03. User copy cannot claim a record exists until it does.
- Cancellation/expiry/failure do not imply refunds are complete or donated items returned. Recovery objects must separately show reconciliation and custody progress. A delayed provider capture remains reconcilable after a terminal mission state.
- COMPLETED, CANCELLED, FAILED and EXPIRED have no reopen command in this draft. Later challenges create a case/correction against historical facts; no overwrite of final verified history. DISPUTED is nonterminal and has only proposed reviewed exits M21/M22.
- During PAUSED, no new contribution/assignment authorization. Deadline handling during pause and safe continuation of physical custody need PD-12. Neither scheduler nor org may blindly restore the saved state after its guards cease to hold.
- Money closure cannot close open item/time CTAs. Need-level availability is evaluated with current org, mission, reservations, target revisions and eligibility. No overfill policy is inferred from `allow_partial`.
- Global mission completion, contribution completion and organization payout are distinct facts. “Received by organization” requires evidenced money payout, not M03/M08/M12 or provider capture.
