# Design assets and token handoff

2026-09-13. [Machine-readable tokens](tokens.json) refine the design brief's semantic colors for measurable contrast. [Contrast evidence](contrast-evidence.json) is generated by `python tools/check_design_tokens.py --write`; ordinary checks omit `--write`. These are opaque color-pair measurements, not a screen-reader, whole-screen accessibility or usability pass.

Light placeholders and timestamps use a darker readable tertiary token. Warning/error/info colors have separate readable text uses. Dark primary buttons use dark `onPrimary` text instead of assuming white will contrast. `decorativeDivider` is never the sole control boundary or focus indicator; use `controlBorder` and `focus`. Disabled state must retain understandable labels and explain why action is unavailable. Status always includes text/icon, not color alone.

The supplied board was visually inspected on 2026-09-13: ten screens and the butterfly/serif wordmark establish warm surfaces, forest-green actions, editorial headings, cards and five-tab navigation. The wallet amount, non-Delhi examples, absolute contribution claim, unverified impact counters and immediate Share Impact success action require the documented MVP corrections. The board supplies visual direction, not cleared image/font rights or implementation evidence.

## Layout and interaction handoff

Use 16 px horizontal padding below 360 px, 20 px otherwise. Layouts reflow at 320/360/390/412 px, with no fixed-height text container. Controls meet 44 px targets (48 px primary); long labels wrap and sheet/footer content stays reachable at 200% text. Portals reflow tables to labeled row summaries when columns cannot fit; preserve row identity and action context.

Focus rings use a 3 px indicator and 3 px offset. Modal flows trap and restore focus, expose a label, and handle Escape/back without falsely cancelling uncertain provider work. Form errors attach to fields and focus an error summary; state announcements occur on meaningful changes only. Motion respects the reduced-motion setting and uses at most 150 ms fades there.
